About Me —

My introduction to design didn’t begin with pixels—it began with watching my brother design an international convention centre as part of his architecture graduation project. I observed him work through drawings, 3D models, and finally prepare for his jury. And something clicked—or perhaps short-circuited. Although I had an interest in computers, the sheer intentionality behind every decision—the scale, the process, the idea that spaces can influence how people feel—left me both inspired and quietly overwhelmed. I realised then that design wasn’t just about how things look; it was about how they work—for people, for moments, and for what comes next.

I went on to study Computer Science and graduated in 2013. Toward the end of my bachelor’s, I realised computer science wasn’t entirely my thing. What excited me more was the intersection of design and code and Front End Engineering came naturally to me. I started learning HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and soon found myself designing and building websites. I kept learning, designing, breaking things, fixing them, reading blogs, and staying up way too late experimenting. No curriculum, no grades—just curiosity and the grit to chase great design.

Today, I'm a self-taught Product Designer with a strong focus on user research, product strategy, and visual design. I see design as a multidisciplinary craft where empathy meets logic, and creativity shakes hands with constraint. It's less about pushing pixels, more about solving problems and asking the right questions (and occasionally, the wrong ones too—just to be sure).

Outside of work, I read on need basis, meditate inconsistently, admire good typography like it's fine art, and chase thoughts around the internet like a digital squirrel. I'm deeply curious about art, spirituality, stoicism, karma yoga, and what Iceland looks like in winter. My playlists swing between EDMs, DHH, Indian classical and 90s Bollywood songs. Some of my all time favourite artists are Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan, Jagjit Singh, Bob Dylan, Eddie Vedder, Sigur Rós, Ólafur Arnalds, and Krishna Das.

Still learning. Still tinkering. Still asking why not.
