Designing Rest in Chaos: A Sleep POD for the Sleepless in Cities

IIT Bombay • Certificate Course • 15 Days

As part of an intensive HCI course at IDC, IIT Bombay, I conducted contextual research to understand what disrupts sleep in metro cities, ranging from noise to light to stress triggers. These insights informed the design of a concept Sleep POD aimed at improving sleep quality in urban environments. The result was a human-centered, sensory-aware prototype designed to restore rest in overstimulated city life.
