About Me

While initially pursuing an engineering career, the intersection of design, technology, and people is what drove me into the product design space. I'm passionate about bringing creative thinkers together to solve complex problems — and making sure to have fun while at it!

Currently, I'm working to build the #1 digital clinic for joint and muscle pain through design at Hinge Health. As a Staff Product Designer, I'm focused on leading the design and strategy of our mobile app experience, delivering our patients the best possible experience to discover, engage, and start their treatment journey.

Between the hours of 5pm - 9am, I often freelance and consult for start-ups and independent brands. On weekends, you can find me checking off a never-ending list of travel destinations, playing tennis, pursuing local Thai spots, or designing interiors.
